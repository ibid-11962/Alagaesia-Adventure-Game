**The mountain city under Farthen Dûr, Tronjheim**

*We are all in grave danger,* said Ajihad, leader of the Varden. *An army of Urgals is approaching, maybe a day's march from here. But the Urgals are not approaching over land: They are in the tunnels . . . we are going to be attacked from below.*

*Our only hope is to contain the Urgals in three of the larger tunnels and channel them into Farthen Dûr so they don't swarm inside the city of Tronjheim like locusts. I need you to help the dwarves collapse extraneous tunnels.*

*This is one battle we cannot lose. Now go and complete your task!*
